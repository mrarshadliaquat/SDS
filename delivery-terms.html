Delivery Terms & Conditions

These terms explain how delivery is arranged for purchases placed through Averon and how customers and sellers are protected during fulfilment.

Effective: 27 May 2026

1. Delivery coverage

Availability, delivery time and fees are displayed at checkout and may differ by item, seller, delivery address and courier coverage. A delivery order is confirmed only after the checkout confirmation is issued.

2. Fulfilled by Averon

Items marked Fulfilled by Averon are handed to an approved delivery partner under Averon coordination. Other items may be delivered by an approved seller or courier, as shown in the order details.

3. Delivery estimates and tracking

Estimated dates are not guaranteed delivery times. Weather, access restrictions, public holidays and courier disruption may affect delivery. Customers can request status assistance through Averon Support using their order reference.

4. Charges and payment

Applicable shipping fees and payment methods are shown before the order is submitted. Cash on Delivery is payable at receipt where offered. Card or wallet payments remain subject to payment confirmation.

5. Receiving an order

Customers should provide a complete address and reachable contact information, check the package condition on receipt, and report missing, damaged or incorrect items promptly through customer support.

6. Failed or refused delivery

A delivery may be rescheduled or returned when the address is incomplete, the recipient is unavailable, payment cannot be completed, or delivery is refused. Any refund eligibility follows the applicable returns and refund policy.

7. Support and disputes

Delivery questions can be sent through the live Averon Support panel on the customer website. Keep the order number and delivery details available so the support team can investigate correctly.

8. Updates

Averon may revise these terms when delivery services, legal obligations or payment arrangements change. The version effective at the time of order remains relevant to that order.
